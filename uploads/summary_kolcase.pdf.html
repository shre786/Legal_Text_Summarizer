As horrific details have emerged in the course of media reportage, t he brutality of the sexual assault and the nature of the crime have shocked the conscience of the Nation. Writ petitions were instituted before the Calcutta High Court seeking among other things, a court -monitored investigation of the crime and the conduct of the hospital authorities, including the role of the Principal of the medical college and other officials by a special team of investigating officers. Medical Associations have consistently raised issues o f the lack of workplace safety in health care institutions. Hospitals and medical care facilities are open throughout the day and night.
